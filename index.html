CineVerity

Agentic AI technical direction for physically grounded cinema.

CineVerity turns cinematic creative intent into a traceable, physically aware scene plan through specialist AI stages that preserve evidence, uncertainty, physical constraints, and deliberate artistic deviations.

Explore implemented stagesLocal setupOpen repository ↗
Design principle
Creative freedom + technical accountability
Reality may be bent intentionally — not accidentally.
01 · Product boundary

From artistic intent to traceable decisions.

CineVerity keeps creative direction, evidence, physical assessment, production planning, and readiness distinct—so uncertainty and intentional departures from realism remain visible.
DIRECTOR

Structure the brief

Director creates the structured boundary for creative context, scene entities, material unknowns, physical questions, research requirements, and validation targets.

RESEARCH

Ground the evidence

Bounded deterministic search planning drives Parallel Search API retrieval; provenance is preserved before Gemini synthesizes the supplied evidence snapshot.

CONSTRAINTS

Keep deviations explicit

Physical Constraints distinguishes supported, conditional, conflicting, unsupported, indeterminate, unresolved, and deliberate artistic deviations.

02 · Specialist stages implemented

One creative brief. Controlled specialist boundaries.

These stages have controlled runners and validated contracts. They are currently run manually; there is no single automatic end-to-end orchestrator yet.

Core question

Can an AI system help filmmakers bend reality intentionally, rather than accidentally?

1
LLM generation is nondeterministic. Acceptance is deterministic.Validated upstream contracts constrain one model candidate before deterministic code decides accept or reject.
2
Valid JSON is not valid provenance.Structural validation is followed by exact provenance and scope fidelity checks.
3
Wrong snapshots do not silently pass.A structurally valid artifact from mismatched upstream inputs is rejected rather than treated as authoritative.
