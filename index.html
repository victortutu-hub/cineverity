CineVerity

Agentic AI technical direction for physically grounded cinema.

CineVerity is being built to transform cinematic creative intent into an evidence-grounded production plan — connecting research, physical constraints, scene design, and validation without hiding uncertainty.

Design principle
Creative freedom + technical accountability
Reality may be bent intentionally — not accidentally.
01 · Vision

From artistic intent to defensible decisions.

A director may know what a scene should feel like while the technical path remains fragmented across optics, materials, lighting, atmosphere, simulation, and current research. CineVerity explores an agentic layer between those worlds.
INTENT

Preserve the cinematic goal

Start from the filmmaker's brief rather than forcing the creative idea into a rigid technical template.

EVIDENCE

Ground technical claims

Runtime research is intended to connect recommendations to traceable external evidence instead of plausible-sounding guesswork.

VALIDATION

Expose contradictions

Separate physically supported behavior, uncertainty, and deliberate artistic departures so decisions remain inspectable.

Core question

Can an AI system help filmmakers bend reality intentionally, rather than accidentally?

1
Do not confuse plausibility with truth.Technical confidence should follow evidence, not language-model fluency.
2
Keep art and physics distinct but connected.A deliberate stylization is valid when the departure from realism is explicit.
3
Make the reasoning path inspectable.Research, constraints, planning, and validation are separate stages by design.
